Location: server/domains/finance/paymentGateways.ts:1051, 1081, server/domains/finance/finance.routes.ts:2180
Root Cause: When creating a PayPal checkout session, custom_id and invoice_id are set to the internal checkout ID (chk_...), while PayPal's Order ID (8AB...) is saved in provider_tx_id.
